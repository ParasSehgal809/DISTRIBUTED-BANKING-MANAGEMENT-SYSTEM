ADBMS Concept: Vertical Fragmentation & Distributed Join Reconstruction

The Customer entity is partitioned vertically to satisfy security and locality constraints:

Fragment 1 (Central Coordinator DB): customers_global(customer_id, full_name, email, phone, gov_id_type, gov_id_number, home_address)
Fragment 2 (Branch Node DB): customers_local(customer_id, kyc_status, credit_score, occupation, annual_income, risk_rating)

Reconstruction Algorithm: GlobalCatalog.reconstruct_all_customers() queries customers_global on the coordinator, dispatches queries to branch nodes for financial profiles, and performs an in-memory hash join on customer_id.
